\section{Conclusion}

GeoProbe measures how geolocation predictions change under controlled,
region-conditioned attention interventions. On Img2GPS3k, the
schedule selected on discovery gave no supported held-out gain, yet moved
predictions by $189.5$~km at mean cosine $0.9984$. Detected regions changed
the embedding, and suppressed boxes the top-1 prediction, more than matched
controls, without improving accuracy or showing that the content explains the
prediction. Evaluation covers one model and benchmark,
without users; future work adds matched controls to the interface, further
models and benchmarks, and a user study.
